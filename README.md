🎬 YouTube Viral Clip Generator

Automatically turn long-form YouTube videos into 10 viral-ready short clips using Google Colab and store them directly in your Google Drive.

🚀 How to Use This Repository

Follow these simple steps to set up and run the script:

1. Download the Script

Download the script file from this repository to your local computer.

2. Open Google Colab & Upload

Go to Google Colab.

Click on File > Upload notebook (or create a new notebook and upload the script).

Select the script file you just downloaded from your system.

3. Add Your YouTube Video Link

Locate the input field in the notebook and paste the URL of the YouTube video you want to clip.

Run the notebook cells.

4. Connect Your Google Drive

As the script runs, it will prompt you to authorize and connect your Google Drive.

Grant the necessary permissions—this is where your generated clips will be saved automatically.

5. Wait for Processing

Sit back while the script downloads, analyzes, and slices the video.

Note: Processing time will vary depending on the length of the video and your internet connection speed.

6. Download & Upload!

Once finished, open your connected Google Drive to find your 10 newly generated viral clips.

Simply download them to your device and upload them directly to YouTube Shorts (or your preferred social media platform)!

💡 Tips for Best Results

Stable Connection: Ensure you have a steady internet connection while the notebook is running so Google Colab doesn't disconnect.

Drive Storage: Make sure you have enough free space in your Google Drive to store the output video files.
